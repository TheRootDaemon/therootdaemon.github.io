<br>

This one was **_really addictive_**. I found myself wanting to read
the **_life stories_** of people who **_perceive money_** in different ways.

Here are some **_quotes_**, rather **_little phrases_** that just stuck with me:

<br>

- No one's **_crazy_**.
- Nothing is as good or as bad as it seems.
- Progress happens too **_slowly_**, but setbacks happen too **_quickly_** to ignore.
- **_Spending_** money to show people how much money you have is the **_fastest_** way to have **_less money_**.
- Things that have **_never happened_** before **_happen all the time_**.
- Doing **_well_** with money has a **_little_** to do with how **_smart_** you are **_and a lot to do_** with how you behave.

<br>

Of all of them, **_Nothing is as good or as bad as it seems_** really stuck with me.
It just hit me on the spot.

Uhmm, as for the part where they talked about the
**_hedge funds_**, **_dividends_** ie., the **_financial jargons_**,
well, I was too dumb to perceive that :)

And finally, I read this **_really nice book_** which my colleague was kind enough to lend me.
What a **_steal_** !
