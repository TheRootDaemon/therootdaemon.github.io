<br>

I took **_way too long_** to read this book.

I felt like it started off slow, with a steady pace.
I was going steady until he was at the Crystal Shop,
where he worked and saved up for his trip.

Then after that I felt **_sleepy_** !

Not once not twice but **_many times_**. Naturally I postponed reading it.

And it all changed when he entered the **_Al-Fayoum Oasis_**.
I loved it after that, there was a **_nice romance_**, philosophy
and **_plot twist_**, I truly didn't expect that !

It was definitely worth my **_money_** :)
